What Genius Switch is

Genius Switch is a digital audio series you listen to with headphones. There are no pills or devices, just recordings you play once a day.

The creators pitch it as a way to "rev up" brain power, sharpen focus and help memory. It's a sound-based listening routine, not a medical treatment.

The idea behind it

The official page leans on two research topics. Here's what they are in plain words.

40Hz sound

40Hz is a gamma brainwave frequency. Some studies have looked at 40Hz tones and binaural beats for attention and creativity, and researchers are still exploring it.

BDNF

BDNF is a protein linked to learning and memory. The vendor cites a long list of BDNF studies, but those studies aren't tests of this particular audio.

In short, the science it points to is real. Whether this specific recording does anything for you is something you'll only find out by trying it. That's why the guarantee matters.

How you use it

  1. Put on headphones

    Binaural-style audio relies on each ear getting its own sound.

  2. Find a quiet few minutes

    Mornings or before deep work are easy slots to protect.

  3. Listen daily

    Treat it like a habit, the same way you'd treat meditation or a walk.

  4. Judge it within 90 days

    Notice how your focus feels, and use the guarantee if it isn't for you.
